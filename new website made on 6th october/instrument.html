Opto-mechanical system

Instrument architecture

EXOhSPEC combines fibre-fed high-resolution spectroscopy with interferometric path metrology, environmental sensing, detector centroiding and two-timescale feedback.

Optical path

Light enters through a fibre feed, is dispersed by the spectrograph optics and recorded by the science camera. A reference feature is tracked on the detector so that horizontal and vertical motion can be expressed in pixels. The IDS3010 interferometer records optical-path-length change in parallel.

Stabilisation interfaces

The Meerstetter TEC controller changes the thermal operating point slowly. A tip–tilt active-optics unit supplies faster, bounded detector-plane correction. Environmental sensors and four-region PT100/PT104 measurements provide context for both loops.
