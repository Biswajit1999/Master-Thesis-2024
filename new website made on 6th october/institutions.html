NARIT and TNT · Thailand

Partner development and deployment context

NARIT describes a compact fibre-fed spectrograph using prisms and a grating and states that a prototype has been installed and tested with the 2.4 m Thai National Telescope. Its observatory equipment listing also describes EXOhSPEC as under construction, so the exact commissioning milestone remains unresolved.

Telescope guiding and internal stabilisation

The acquisition and guiding unit holds a star image on the fibre at the telescope. The CLFCD work measures and corrects behaviour inside the laboratory spectrograph. They are different control problems.

Design targets and measured results
