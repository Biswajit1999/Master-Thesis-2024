UK–Thailand astronomical instrumentation programme

Compact high-resolution spectroscopy, from optical design to active stability.

EXOhSPEC is a collaborative research and development programme spanning fibre-fed echelle design, illumination, calibration, telescope acquisition, laboratory stability measurements and active control. It connects a University of Hertfordshire test bed with partner development and telescope testing in Thailand.

A programme of prototypes, instrument contexts and research activities—not one interchangeable hardware configuration.

02 / Programme

One platform, connected research

A spectrograph is only as precise as the whole measurement chain.

EXOhSPEC treats the optics, fibre feed, detector, environment, calibration and control as coupled parts of one instrument. The programme moves between optical simulation, laboratory characterisation and telescope deployment.

At the telescope

A laboratory platform connected to the sky.

The acquisition and guiding unit couples a stellar image at the Thai National Telescope focal plane into an optical fibre while an internal camera supports closed-loop guiding. EXOhSPEC has recorded early astronomical spectra and radial-velocity measurements.
