03 / Research contribution

Nearly three years of learning and iteration

The research did not begin at V17.

The public record separates work that pre-dated Biswajit Jana, the systems he inherited and learned, the interfaces and experiments he modified or integrated, and the later investigations reported in his research archive.

2023–2024Understand

Learn the instrument and trust the measurement

ThAr fibre illumination, detector acquisition, IDS3010 optical-path measurement, environmental sensing, refractive-index effects and the MSc control question.

  • Inherited the wider collaborative spectrograph programme
  • Mapped optical, thermal and software dependencies
  • Separated OPL, detector motion and environmental observables
2025Measure and debug

Build a usable data and control path

MaxIm DL/Python acquisition, unstable COM interfaces, AO direction and hysteresis tests, passive baselines, adaptive/fixed PID, 2 mK steps and cooldown logic.

  • Software failures became experimental evidence
  • Thermal delay ruled out rapid repeated correction
  • Baseline and feedback windows needed explicit definitions
Early 2026Control and learn from failure

Test model transport, bias and actuator limits

Long hybrid runs A–E, June V4 and component perturbations showed that a controller could reduce RMS yet retain bias, break away or exhaust bounded AO travel.

  • Negative runs remain visible
  • Reference selection became a first-class result
  • Local heating separated image and OPL sensitivities
Mid–Oct 2026Endure, refine, diagnose

From V11 and V17 endurance to a physical hypothesis

V11–V17 established interval-specific containment, V18 improved registration, and V19–V24 traced coupled room, chiller, camera and optical behaviour before the proposed V25 airflow test.

  • Long duration and short-window precision are not ranked as one metric
  • Thermal-only runs are labelled separately
  • V25 remains unvalidated until powered testing
Authorship boundary: this is a supervised research strand within the wider EXOhSPEC programme. Original optical, fibre, mechanical, guiding, software and partner-institution contributions remain credited to their authors and teams.

05 / Current position

What is known—and what is not

The evidence supports a coupled thermal pathway, not a single proven cause.

Demonstrated

Sustained sub-pixel operation

V17 retained every settled measurement inside ±0.5 px across 88.59 h. V18.2 produced the strongest reported short-run dY RMS in this sequence.

Interpreted

Thermal and environmental coupling

V19–V24 narrowed the disturbance to a coupled camera, cooling and environment problem. The runs do not isolate one component as the sole cause.

Proposed / in fabrication

Camera-body homogenisation

V25 tests a low-power push–pull airflow path. No performance improvement is claimed before fit, vibration and thermal/optical validation.
