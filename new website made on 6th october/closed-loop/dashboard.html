01 SYSTEM TOPOLOGY

Measurements remain separate until the supervisory decision.

Detector displacement, optical-path length and environmental telemetry are different observables. The controller interprets them together but does not report one as a substitute for another.

SYSTEM MAP / REPORT-DERIVED ARCHITECTURE ARCHIVE SNAPSHOT
  1. ThAr spectrum recorded as CCD/FITS frames.
  2. Phase correlation estimates dX, dY and radial error.
  3. IDS3010, PT104, BME680 and LK220 channels provide context.
  4. Supervisory logic applies validity gates and actuator limits.
  5. TEC carries slow drift; active optics trims bounded residuals.
  6. The next exposure verifies the resulting detector state.
