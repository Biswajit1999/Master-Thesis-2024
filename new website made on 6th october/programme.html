Research record

A chronological narrative plus the underlying PDF collection assembled from the thesis repository, Downloads and reports sent to Prof Bill Martin and Prof Hugh Jones.

Programme storyline

The sequence shows how the work moved from instrument planning to calibration, environmental modelling, hybrid feedback and targeted diagnostics. It also records where interpretations changed.

2023

Project definition

Initial plan, measurement questions and thesis scope: relate laboratory environment to spectrograph stability.

2024

Baseline characterisation

Thesis work established the acquisition path, environmental correlations, AO response and early feedback concept.

2025

Metrology and feedback experiments

Interferometer infrastructure, OPL equations and model development were integrated with detector-centroid feedback experiments.

Jan–Apr 2026

Stage-2 long-run control

Longer experiments tested reference management, thresholding and stability intervals; V17 consolidated controller lineage and recomputed metrics from raw CSV data.

May–Jul 2026

Calibration and local thermal tests

AO direction/scale calibration, environmental compensation, 44.5 h thermal regulation and controlled local heat inputs separated actuator and disturbance responses.

Aug–Sep 2026

Thermal-path diagnosis

V19–V21 investigated repeating thermal behaviour; later reports narrowed claims and proposed V25 airflow homogenisation as a testable design, not a validated fix.

Oct 2026

Method comparison and consolidated record

Phase correlation was benchmarked against centroid tracking, and the website/archive was reorganised for external scientific review.
