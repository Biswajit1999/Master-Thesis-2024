03 / Heat load ≠ variability

V22–V23 inference

Turning the camera TEC off reduced load—but did not solve stability.

01

Heat load established

In V23 the coolant temperature rise reduced from about 0.3095 °C to 0.1143 °C and LK220 current fell from 2.749 A to 2.065 A when the camera TEC was switched off.

02

Outlet variation remained

Outlet-temperature sigma changed only from 0.0610 °C to 0.0615 °C. Removing camera heat did not remove the repeating outlet variability.

03

Optical transition unacceptable

The detector warmed and the image underwent a large thermo-optical transition. Camera TEC OFF is an intervention for diagnosis, not an operating solution.

V24 observation

Optical observables returned after camera rotation

Over the early ~28 min interval, dY reached about +0.64 px while OPL changed by about −0.32 µm. The detector and coolant remained close to their controlled temperatures, but external camera regions did not behave as one isothermal body.

Mechanical settling, room forcing and thermal redistribution remained coupled, so this observation motivates V25 without proving causality.
