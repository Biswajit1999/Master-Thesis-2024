# Member access

Restricted EXOhSPEC working material is no longer served directly from this public repository.

Use **Member** on the [EXOhSPEC website](https://biswajit1999.github.io/EXOhSPEC-website/) to sign in with a Google account. The research owner has automatic access; other researchers remain pending until the owner approves their account. Approved members can then open the protected closed-loop research dashboard, report reader and controlled files.

No unpublished reports, experimental logs or restricted datasets are intentionally published from this repository.
