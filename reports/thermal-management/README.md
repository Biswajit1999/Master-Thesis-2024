# Thermal-management reports

This folder records ongoing EXOhSPEC thermal-management studies that extend the original MSc feedback-control work.

| Report | Scope |
|---|---|
| [V25 camera thermal-homogenisation and dual side-vent fan mount](EXOhSPEC_V25_camera_thermal_homogenisation.md) | Motivation from post-rotation thermal/pixel monitoring, compact low-power fan selection, parametric mount design, vibration isolation and staged validation plan. |

These reports describe experimental development. Proposed hardware changes are not treated as improvements until they are validated against thermal, pixel and OPL measurements.
