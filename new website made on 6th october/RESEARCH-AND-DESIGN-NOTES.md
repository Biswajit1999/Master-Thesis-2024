# EXOhSPEC website research and design notes

## Reference sites reviewed on 6 October 2026

- [EXOhSPEC official site](https://star.herts.ac.uk/exohspec/) — source hierarchy, programme imagery, technical documents and original publication list.
- [ESPRESSO at ESO](https://www.eso.org/sci/facilities/paranal/instruments/espresso.html) — separation of overview, instrument description, science, manuals, tools and operations.
- [NEID at WIYN](https://www.wiyn.org/Instruments/wiynneid_call.html) — concise engineering characteristics, system performance and the connection between telescope interface, controlled environment, calibration and data products.
- [CARMENES instrument](https://carmenes.caha.es/ext/instrument/index.html) and [timeline](https://carmenes.caha.es/ext/timeline/index.html) — specification tables, programme milestones, instrument gallery and publication/thesis separation.
- [Keck Planet Finder](https://keckobservatory.org/our-story/telescopes/keck-planet-finder/) — clear science purpose, differentiating engineering feature and instrument identity.

## Applied design decisions

1. Use a multi-page programme architecture instead of one long page.
2. Keep the main EXOhSPEC programme broader than any one thesis.
3. Present specifications with scope labels such as “design” or “prototype”.
4. Give the closed-loop work its own EXOhSPEC-CLFCD research track.
5. Separate measured results, demonstrated intervals, interpretations, open objectives and proposed designs.
6. Use programme photographs on the main site and thesis-derived internal images only inside CLFCD.
7. Make the programme and research timelines operable with buttons and keyboard focus, with a static reduced-motion state.
8. Use a light institutional palette, a custom vector mark, restrained spectral-flow motion and no scroll-jacking.
9. Route the expanded CLFCD record through dedicated results, thermal-diagnostic, prototype and evidence pages instead of turning the programme homepage into a thesis archive.
10. Treat V25 as a product-development page with verified dimensions and public CAD, while labelling it explicitly as an unvalidated prototype.
11. Make tabbed research sequences keyboard-operable with arrow, Home and End keys, and keep every data table horizontally accessible on small screens.
